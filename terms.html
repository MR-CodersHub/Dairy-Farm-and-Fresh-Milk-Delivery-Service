By placing an order, starting a subscription or using this website, you agree to the terms below. They are written to be read, not skipped. If anything is unclear, the care desk is happy to talk it through.

1. Acceptance

These terms apply to all orders and website use. When you create an account, tick an agreement box or place an order, you confirm that you have read and accept them. If you do not agree, please do not place an order. We may update these terms from time to time; the current version is always on this page with its review date.

2. Our products

Everything we sell is fresh, made in small batches and delivered in returnable glass or reusable tubs. Because it is real dairy, appearance and taste can vary slightly with the season and the farm. Product descriptions and images are guides, not guarantees of an exact appearance. If a listed item is briefly unavailable, we will tell you and offer a suitable substitute before your order goes out.

3. Delivery

Deliveries arrive between 5:30 AM and 6:30 AM on your chosen days, within the pincodes we serve. Please make sure someone can receive the order and that the delivery spot is accessible. If we cannot reach your address, we will contact you and may return the bottles to the farm. Delivery times are a window, not an exact minute — the route is affected by weather and traffic like any other morning road.

4. Orders, pricing and payment

Prices are shown on the website before you confirm an order and include delivery within our core area. Subscription prices include the stated saving. We do not add surprise fees at checkout. Payment is collected through our payment provider; by ordering you confirm that you are authorised to use the payment method provided. If a payment fails, we will pause the affected delivery until it is settled.

5. Pause, skip and cancel

You can pause, skip a day, change quantities or cancel a subscription in your dashboard, or by calling the care desk, up to 9 PM the evening before the next delivery. Changes made after that cutoff may apply from the following delivery day. There is no lock-in and no cancellation fee. Billing adjusts automatically when you pause or skip.

6. Glass bottles and returns

Our bottles belong to the farm and are meant to come back. Please rinse them and leave them at your door on your next delivery day; the rider collects them and we wash and refill them. Lost or badly damaged bottles may be charged at a reasonable replacement cost. Tell us about a damaged seal, a leak or a bottle that did not arrive cold and we will replace it without a question.
