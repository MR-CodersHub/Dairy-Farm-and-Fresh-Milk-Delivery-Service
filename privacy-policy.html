Our promise Never sold. Never shared.

This policy covers the Meadowly Dairy Co. website, our morning delivery service and the care desk. We keep it short on purpose. If anything here is unclear, write to us and we will explain it in ordinary words.

1. Who we are

Meadowly Dairy Co. is a small dairy based on Anaikatti Road, Coimbatore 641 108, India. We bottle milk at dawn and deliver it in returnable glass. For anything relating to this policy, contact care@meadowly.in or call +91 422 400 1880.

2. What we collect

We only ask for what helps us get a bottle to your door and answer your questions:

  • Account and contact details — name, phone number, email address and delivery address.
  • Delivery preferences — pincode, chosen products, quantities, pause dates and delivery notes.
  • Order and payment status — what you ordered and whether it was paid for. We do not store full card numbers; payments are handled by our payment provider.
  • Messages — questions, requests and feedback you send to the care desk.
  • Basic technical data — browser type, pages visited and approximate location from your IP, used only to keep the site working and understand what people actually read.

3. How we use it

We use your information to create and manage deliveries, answer support requests, process subscriptions, send service updates you have asked for, improve the website and the morning route, and meet any legal or safety obligations. We do not use your details for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Who sees it

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share only what is necessary with:

  • delivery riders and route staff, who see the address and notes needed for that morning;
  • payment and accounting providers, who process transactions under their own privacy terms;
  • technology providers who host our website and care desk, bound by confidentiality;
  • authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

5. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies keep your session, cart and preferences working. We use a small amount of privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are useful. We do not use advertising pixels, cross-site trackers or sell audience data. You can block cookies in your browser; the site will still load, though some preferences may not be remembered.

6. Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold, correct anything that is wrong, update your delivery details in the dashboard, pause or cancel a subscription, or ask us to delete your account and associated data. Newsletter emails include an unsubscribe link in every issue. We may keep minimal records where the law requires it, but we will explain that if it applies.

7. How long we keep it

We keep account and delivery information while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for billing and support. Data that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised. You can ask us to start that process at any time.

8. Security

We use sensible technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including limited staff access, encrypted connections and careful handling of addresses. No online service can promise perfect security, so please tell us quickly if you think something is wrong with your account.

9. Children

Our service and website are not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. If a parent or guardian believes a child has shared details with us, please contact the care desk and we will remove them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we will also let active customers know by email or in the dashboard before the change takes effect.

11. Contact us

Questions, corrections and deletion requests all go to the same place: care@meadowly.in, or +91 422 400 1880 between 7 AM and 8 PM every day. We reply within one working day.
